Direct Questions — Key Points

Why Google?

Android = my craft (DETE→Fiorano→DevRev→RouteChef 50K). Scale: 50 clients → billions. Build FOR platform: Jetpack, Compose, Play Services. Culture: code reviews + testing + design docs = norm. Growth: L4→L5, learn from people who built my tools.

Why leaving DevRev?

Same product 2+ years. Grateful for growth. Ready for bigger scale. Want to build FOR the platform, not just ON it.

3-5 year vision

Stronger engineer, lead small team, meaningful difference on product used by millions. L4→L5 = deeper ownership + broader influence.

Biggest weakness

Over-engineering. DETE: perfected arch, ignored business model. Mitigation: timebox exploration, validate with users in week 1.

Negative feedback

Shipped regression in legacy SDK, survived 3 releases. Manager direct feedback. Full ownership → pre-ship checklist → regression tests for every change. Led to testing culture at Fiorano.

Wrong technical decision

DETE: no MVVM, multi-Activity spaghetti. College mentor reviewed → pointed out structural problems. Refactored to MVVM → maintainable for 15 contributors. Strong opinions, loosely held.

Favorite Google product

Android Studio / Jetpack Compose. Use daily, understand the craft, want to contribute to making them better for every Android dev.

Coffee with Pichai

SDK dev experience on Android: view hierarchy + accessibility APIs need to be more SDK-friendly. Java→Kotlin migration paths: structured concurrency, null safety, Flow solve real production issues (OOM, races, NPE).

Google's mission

"Organize world's info, make accessible." = reduce friction between people and what they need. Android: billions of devices. DETE: merchants reached customers they never had access to.

Biggest Google challenge

AI transition into products without losing trust/simplicity. Android: fragmentation evolving — foldables, cars, watches, TV. Compose + Jetpack across all form factors + on-device AI.

Handle stress

Separate controllable vs. not. DETE: startup + placements + education loan → time blocks. DevRev SDK stalled → vertical slices to reduce overwhelm. Cricket + Valorant = reset buttons.

Time to contribute

30 days: codebase, norms, ship small fix. 90 days: own a feature e2e. 6 months: team-level initiative. Evidence: took over stalled SDK, shipped in weeks.

Define success

Ship something that improves someone's workflow AND makes codebase better. Crash fix: improved every user's experience. MVVM: made every future feature faster. Impact that compounds.

Smartest person I know

College mentor who taught me Android. Looked at DETE spaghetti code, immediately saw structural problems + explained WHY they'd cause pain. Thinks in systems, not code.

Career over again?

Same path but start DETE with a business co-founder. Validate business model before writing code. But wouldn't trade DETE — taught more about leadership + trade-offs than any job.

Ultimate power

Quality software education in tier-2/3 Indian cities. College-industry gap is massive. DETE proof: 2 team members got Android jobs. Mentorship access would explode the talent pipeline.
